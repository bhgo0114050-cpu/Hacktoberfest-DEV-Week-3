LazyDesk 💤

Your personal workspace for doing less busywork and getting more done.

LazyDesk is a productivity workspace designed to simplify everyday tasks, reduce digital clutter, and help you stay focused without feeling overwhelmed. From organizing your to-do list to managing focus sessions, LazyDesk brings essential productivity tools together in one clean, user-friendly dashboard.

✨ Features

- 📊 Productivity Dashboard — Get a quick overview of your tasks, progress, and daily productivity.
- ✅ Smart To-Do List — Add, complete, delete, and prioritize tasks to keep your day organized.
- 📁 File Organizer — Explore a categorized view of files and keep digital clutter under control.
- 📥 Inbox Organizer — View and categorize items in an organized inbox-style interface.
- ⏱️ Focus Mode — Use a focus timer to work in dedicated sessions and take breaks when needed.
- 🌿 Relax Mode — Take a breather with calming prompts and a more relaxed workspace experience.
- 💾 Persistent Preferences — Keep supported tasks and settings saved between visits.
- 📱 Responsive Interface — Enjoy a clean layout across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
- 🎨 Minimal Design — A distraction-free interface that makes productivity feel simple rather than stressful.

💡 Why LazyDesk?

Modern digital life often involves too many tabs, scattered tasks, overflowing inboxes, and constant distractions. LazyDesk brings useful organizational tools into one place so users can spend less time managing their workflow and more time doing what matters.

The idea is simple: work smarter, stay organized, and make room to breathe.
